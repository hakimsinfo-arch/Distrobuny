# Distro Buny — Name and Branding Policy

## 1. Purpose

Distro Buny is an independent, free and open-source software project.

This policy explains how the Distro Buny name, logo, and visual identity may be used while helping users distinguish official releases from independent projects.

## 2. Permitted Uses

We welcome accurate references to Distro Buny in:

- Reviews, tutorials, and educational content
- Videos, documentaries, and livestreams
- News articles, blogs, and technical discussions
- Comparisons and demonstrations
- Community-created content and projects

Creators may identify Distro Buny by name and use its logo when reasonably necessary to discuss or illustrate the original project, provided the use is not misleading.

Independent content does not require endorsement by Distro Buny, and creators must not suggest official approval where none exists.

## 3. Modified Versions

Distro Buny's applicable software licenses permit modification and redistribution under their terms.

Modified versions must not be presented as official Distro Buny releases unless authorized by the project.

Developers of modified versions should use distinct names and branding. Truthful statements such as "Based on Distro Buny" are welcome when they do not imply an official relationship.

This branding policy does not restrict the rights granted by the applicable open-source software licenses.

## 4. Misrepresentation

The following uses are not authorized by the project:

- Falsely claiming to be the official Distro Buny developer or project team
- Presenting an unofficial release as an official Distro Buny release
- Falsely claiming sponsorship, endorsement, or partnership
- Using the project's branding to impersonate its official website or distribution channels

These statements are subject to applicable law, including any legally permitted nominative or other uses.

## 5. Commercial Use

Commercial use of Distro Buny software is permitted under its applicable software licenses.

Commercial activity does not automatically authorize the use of Distro Buny's name or logo to imply official endorsement or ownership of the original project.

## 6. Ownership and Third-Party Marks

Rights in the original Distro Buny name, logo, and visual materials are held by their respective lawful owners.

Names and logos belonging to Linux distributions and other third parties remain subject to their owners' rights and policies.
